1.初步形成树图  0-expl_tree.png
执行命令：cd /data1/ytg/medium_models/GA_gpt/EvoMol-master

PATH=/home/szdx/.local/share/mamba/envs/evomol/bin:$PATH \
/home/szdx/.local/share/mamba/envs/evomol/bin/python my_tree_drawing.py \
  --model_path /data1/ytg/medium_models/GA_gpt/0-output/4r6e \
  --layout neato \
  --graphviz_args "-Grankdir=LR -Gnodesep=1.2 -Granksep=1.0" \
  --prop total \
  --neighbours_threshold 9999 \
  --fig_width 24 \
  --fig_height 14 \
  --legend_font_size 6 \
  --legend_offset_x 0.02 \
  --legend_offset_y 0.02 \
  --cmap inferno \
  --no_images
参数代表含义：--prop:使用什么指标来展示颜色变化（右侧柱子的渐变）
